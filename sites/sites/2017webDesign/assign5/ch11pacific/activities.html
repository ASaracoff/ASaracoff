Activities at Pacific Trails

Hiking

Pacific Trails Resot has 5 miles of hiking trails and is adjacent to a state park. Go it alone or join one of our guided hikes.

Kayaking

Ocean kayaks are available for guest use.

Bird Watching

While any time is a good time to go bird watching at Pacific Trails, we offer guided bird watching trips at sunrise several times a week
