Notes

A young orphane girl is the eldest in her orphanage and is used to taking care of kids younger than her, helping out with the day car aswell. She meets with a young group of twins whose parents are missing and are being taken care of their uncle.The uncle is desperate for the girls help to take care of the twins at home because he is a somewhat famous newscaster. Love So Life is the adventures the four of them have in thier daily life. What I enjoy most about this series is the costumes the twins wear omg sooo cute!! theres one that they have tiger suits, and theres another that their squirrels!
