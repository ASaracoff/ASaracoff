Notes

Randou Masashi is a self-centered, brash karate star. One day he becomes a victim of a horrible bus accdent that horribly disfigured his face because of the extensive burns. A genius plastic surgeon, Dr. Manabe, saves Randou's life and went to work recreating his face based on a photo found in the wreckage of the bus. The only problem with this is... the face is the face of Randou's crush! To clarify... he got a girls face!!
