Notes

A cute story about a 27 year old woman who raised her 3 yunger sisters until they got married. After completing this goal she is unexpectedly thrown into an interesting relationship with the new boss at work. They get into a love relationship and he is temporarily blind...and he thinks she is an old grandmother figure!!
