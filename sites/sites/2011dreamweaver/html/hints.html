Helpful Hints

  • Find a Series
  • Page Breakdown

  • If you know the title go to series
  • If you only know art go to gallery
  • If you want to pick a series at random go to gallery
  • Genres give you an understanding of the type of manga/hua/hwa that are out there
  • In the Gallery there are sub-galleries to help you pinpoint your desired next series.
  • When you select a series there are 5 picture links below the general information, 4 are read online links (manga fox/here/park/tank) while the 5th (mangaupdates) is well more information if you want it.
  • If you want more advice on either how to navigate the site or to find a series please email me

  • On ALL pages:
    • in the footer there is my email and on some pages there is the "last updated on" so if you see a current series you can look there to see that last time i modified the page~

  • Genre:
    • You see that big green and pink blob? on the pink part there are genres!
    • Click a genre for a basic break down of what it is
    • For each genre there is a link called ___Gallery.
      • Click that link to see thumbnails of all the series on the site that is in that genre.
    • As well as the general meaning of the genre I have added some examples and/or comments to clarify the genre.

  • Series:
    • Has an explanation in a tab like bar. Select what type you would like to understand.
    • Click the blue underlined word (like Complete for example) to get shot down to that part of the page.
    • This is a list of all the different series that are on the site.
    • Select a title to get to the general information page.
      • In the general info page it gives you: alternative names, author(s), genres, release year, status, type, length, where to read online at, a page where you can go for even more information and a notes section which is comprised of a synopsis and sometimes my input or opinion.
      • The fun feature of the general info pages is that if you want to see a gallery of that series click the picture.

  • Gallery:
    • First thing you see besides the title is the sub-gallery table
      • The sub-gallery table shows the general pages as thummbnails to give you an idea of the series' artwork.
    • Below the sub-gallery table you find the specific gallery.
    • The specific gallery is the thumbnails off all the serires, but if you click the thumbnail you go to a gallery that is just for that series.
      • When on the specific page click on the title of the series to go to it's general info page.
      • You will get a pig picture with little thumbnails below or to it's side. Click the thumbs to see the bigger version.
    • If you want a general overview selest the "General" button in the sub-category table (in the bottom right-hand corner)

  • Links:
    • This site is very basic! It lists all the sites that I have taken my information from and or where to go to purchase, read, or get more information on some series.

  • About:
    • About me, the webmaster, so why I made this site etc~

  • Contact:
    • Email me here
    • Has a form to let me know basic information.
