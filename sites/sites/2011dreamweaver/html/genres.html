Genres

Genres in MMM are very similar to genres in normal literature just adding some more terminology specific for manga, Manhua, and manhwa

Action
Story containing fighting, violence, chaos...pretty much fast paced motion. (pow, crack, light bulb?, bang (where did the light go), kapow, its the end of the series already? What???)

Action Gallery
Adventure
The main character goes on a trip in search of something or someone
(I will embark on a journey through my room in search of my long lost mp3 player! omg i must fight the creature under the bed! SAVE ME!!!)

Adventure Gallery
Comedy
Story contains humor or is satirical in tone, usually has a happy ending.
(um why do i have to explain? do you peoples not know laughing? or....)


Comedy Gallery
Doujinshi
A spin off of the original series, mainly fan based. Like a fan taking original characters and making them do something that they did not do in the original series or anime.(last time i read one it was harry and Malfoy OMG nightmares for a week...my poor mind T.T)
Drama
A work that is meant to bring emotions from the readers. ("sob T.T it made me so emotional! I hate the author" or "my favorite character....got hit by a semi...AGAIN?!?!?!" for example :P)

Drama Gallery
Fantasy
Anything involving, but not limited to, magic, dream worlds, and fairy tales. insert elf eating a giant...either that's a huge elf or the giant is a midget...or both O.o)
Fantasy Gallery
Gender Bender
A character that changes their gender, Through simply wearing male/female clothes, or through magical/medical means depending on the author's intention. (bend the gender man~)

Gender Bender Gallery
Harem
A story that has a guy that is surrounded by females, a female surrounded by many males is known as a reverse harem. (a guys/girls fantasy...surrounded by pretty chicks/dudes)

Harem Gallery
Historical
Takes place in the past or in ancient times(if you don't get it retake some history classes...like 100 or something...or just wiki it!)


Historical Gallery
Horror
Scary manga ~ Simple (did I really have to explain it?)



Horror Gallery
Josei
"Woman" ages 18~30; unlike shoujo, the romance is more realistic and less idealized....also it is more explicit and mature.(ah a women' love ~swoons~)

Josei Gallery
Martial Arts
Anything pertaining to martial arts, like aikido, judo, kendo, karate, tae kwon do, fencing, boxing etc (use your "imagination" (insert spongebob here))

Martial Arts Gallery
Mature
17+ age, may contain violence, foul language, or sexual content
(meaning if you are 16 or younger...get off that page you know better!!)


Mature Gallery
Mystery
there's a mystery and the characters try and solve it... give or take
(you sit down to watch Tv...where is the remote (gasp) a mystery!! who was the last person watching the TV? the remote is in the bag of potato chips??
Mystery Gallery
One Shots
A single chapter or so... a single shot at a series. usually for fillers
(imagine the next chapter you've been waiting for for 3 years...and then instead you get a stupid side story?? *insert fav profanity here* OR your reading the magazine and they say one piece is takin a break insert random story) ____________________________ One Shot Gallery
Psychological
Deals with the state of the mind, usually with abnormal psychology.
(takes you on a mind warp...or was that me on Monster (c) gasp >.>)


Psychological Gallery
Romance
A story that has anything at all to do with love.
(so like all manga chicks read...90% of all manga?)


Romance Gallery
School Life
Story takes place in a school (if you need clarification its usually about people going to school~ so bullying, studying, trips, stealing each others boyfriends, suicide the regular stuff XD)
School Life Gallery
Sci-Fi
Science fiction, involves twists on tech and other science related phenomena which are contrary or stretches of the modern day scientific world.

Sci-fi Gallery
Seinen
young dudes(like totally man) 18~25 years old appeals to university kids and peps who work in the REAL WORLD (gasp) usually deals with issues of adulthood (ewww)

Seinen Gallery

Shoujo Gallery
