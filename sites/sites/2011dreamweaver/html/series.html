Series

There are four different types of series:

  • Complete
  • Current
  • Hiatus
  • One Shots
Complete - a series that has already been totally, completely released and translated/ A series inwhich you can finish in one sitting if you have the time.
Current - the story has not ended yet so you have to wait anywhere between 1 week to 1~2 months for the next chapter. Or the series is complete in the land of origin but is not fully translated yet.
Hiatus - on a break because of the artists health or other issues. These can be anywhere between 1 year and 6+ years....can also come to a point where the artist cannot finish the series which makes it into an unfinished series.
One Shots - series that are only one chapter long, usually a debute of a new artist or a trial to see how the audience would react to specific subject matter. Also can be just a filler for one of the magazines or a side story of a mainstream series.
