About

The purpose of this website is to a) help me keep track of what I, myself, have read and b)to help others to find good new series that may no be so mainstream as the others. This site is catering to what I, as a manga reader, think makes it easy to find a new series, understand basic synopsis and instantly go to a website to start reading. Also the gallery is mainly for those who do not really like lots of text and need to see the artwork before deciding on what to read.

I hope that this site works for your purposes as well as it does to mine. If you wish to give me any suggestions or comments please email me.
