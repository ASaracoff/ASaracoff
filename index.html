Hello! My name is Anna Saracoff and I am a budding Web Developer with many life and diverse work experiences. Thanks to family and life circumstances I have received the opportunity to live in 3 different American States (California, Indiana and Colorado), seen and driven through at least 30 different states and have gotten to explore 13+ different countries such as Mexico, Taiwan, and various countries in Europe.

The opportunity to travel has opened my eyes to the differences between cultures and peoples. What is normal in the States is foreign to Europe - such as free bathrooms. In most places in Europe you need to pay a fee to use a bathroom in a fast food restaurant and other establishments. Another example is that European trains are not equal! From west to east it progressively gets worse. For instance trains in Germany and Switzerland are very nice, you can find plug-ins for your devices and there is usually free wifi; the trains in Hungary are very... "historical" - no electrical outlets or wifi and bathrooms can be rather horrific. Quality wavers depending on where you go and if you pay more for comfort. I bring up these examples up to show that my personal understanding of the things around me has broadened and have birthed a curiosity to explore more and share my findings to others.

I hope that by enhancing my skills in Web and Software Development I can bring my imaginings and findings to life in a way others will appreciate.
